Vignettes

Rendered Quarto vignettes, one structural idea each. Their sources live in the repository's vignettes/ directory.

  1. Getting started: an SIR model, a lockdown and a vaccination, explain, simulate, a Gantt chart.
  2. Composition and conflicts: conflict reports, set-then-scale, caps, ordered policies, associativity, epoch refinement.
  3. One program, many models: AlgebraicPetri, a hand-written ODE and a discrete map with cross-checks and the sampling theorem; companion for AlgebraicDynamics and StockFlow on Julia 1.11.
  4. Define once, stratify for free: an age-stratified SIR by pullback, lift, refinements, and conflicts under merging.
  5. Pulses and flows: transfers, campaigns as flows by augmentation, invariants.
  6. Narratives and queries: persistent and cumulative narratives, the five truth values, Heyting implication.
  7. Inferring interventions: recover a program from a fitted schedule and transport it.
